Buzz Downloader — Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026 · App: Buzz Downloader (com.nuvoralabs.allinonedownloader) · Developer: Nuvora Labs

This policy explains what information Buzz Downloader ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Buzz Downloader has no user accounts. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Your library and downloads. Files you save, their titles, folders, playback positions and download history are stored only on your device.
  • Links and feeds. Links you paste, share to the app or open in the in-app browser, and podcast feeds you follow, are stored on your device. To fetch them, the app connects directly to the website that hosts them; that website receives the normal information any web request contains (such as your IP address).
  • In-app browser. Pages you visit load directly from those websites. Cookies and site data they set are stored on your device. We do not record your browsing history on our servers.
  • Settings and purchase status. Your preferences, your daily download count and whether you own Buzz Pro are stored on your device.

2. Information collected by third-party services

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform Advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad interactions, and your consent choices To show ads (free version), measure them, prevent fraud, and record your privacy choices
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports and diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version, stack traces, and a Firebase installation identifier To find and fix crashes
Firebase Remote Config A Firebase installation identifier and basic app/device information To adjust app settings such as how often ads appear, without an app update
Google Play Billing Purchase information for Buzz Pro, processed by Google Play To sell and restore the one-time Buzz Pro purchase. We never receive your payment details.

These services are provided by Google and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Data sent to them is encrypted in transit.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK or Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buzz Pro removes all ads.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state — to download files and load pages.
  • Notifications — to show download progress and playback controls.
  • Foreground services — so downloads and playback continue when the app is in the background.
  • Advertising ID — used by the ads SDK as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or other files outside the folder you choose.

5. Children

Buzz Downloader is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Data retention and deletion

Everything in section 1 is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days. To ask for deletion of data held by our service providers, contact us below; you can also reset your Advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
